## Description

```text
Bring a little order to a world of color.

Sortue is a relaxing color puzzle where you swap tiles to restore beautiful gradients. Look closely, compare neighboring shades, and watch the colors fall into place.

THREE WAYS TO PLAY

Casual
Set your own pace. Choose a grid size from 4x4 to 12x12, preview the finished gradient, and use hints when you need a little guidance.

Precision
Make every move count. Complete each puzzle within a move limit as the grids grow with your progress.

Pure
Trust your eye for color. Solve without hints, solution previews, or marks that reveal correctly placed tiles.

A NEW BLEND WITH EVERY PUZZLE

Explore soft pastels, rich blues, warm sunset tones, and more. Generated gradients bring fresh color combinations to your next puzzle.

SMALL DETAILS, SATISFYING MOMENTS

• Swap tiles by tapping or dragging.
• Use the fixed corners to guide your arrangement.
• Enjoy ambient music and tactile feedback.
• Play offline on iPhone and iPad.

Whether you want a gentle color break or a closer test of your perception, there is a way to play that fits your mood.

Find your flow. Restore the harmony.
```
